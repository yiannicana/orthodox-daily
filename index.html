About

A daily companion for Orthodox Christians.

✦ What is Daily Orthodoxy?

Daily Orthodoxy is a free, open liturgical companion built to make Orthodox Christian practice more accessible. It features feast days, scripture readings, saint commemorations, fasting guidance, and a growing library of prayers; all in one place, always free.

✦ A note from Yianni

This is a personal project designed and built by me, Yianni. It's something that I'm continuously working on and would love to see be valued by the community.

✦ Liturgical Data

The liturgical calendar (feast titles, saint commemorations, scripture readings, and fasting information) is drawn from the Orthocal API, a free and open-source Orthodox calendar service.

Visit orthocal.info to learn more.

✦ Prayers

The prayers in this app are drawn from the Orologion and Efchologion, as translated in the Book of Prayers: A Selection for Orthodox Christians, published by the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of Australia (2013). They are reproduced here for personal, non-commercial use.
